# Terms page authoring notes

The `/terms` page describes the existing website and Android app, submitted music, optional accounts, guest bearer links, direct invitations, saved cards, acceptable use, provider dependence and the contact address already on the Privacy page. It adds no acceptance gate or acceptance records and makes no claim that merely visiting creates a binding contract. The operator's legal identity and intended legal jurisdiction were not supplied; neither was invented. The page avoids a blanket liability waiver and preserves mandatory rights.

The distinction between an informational terms page and recorded acceptance follows [France Num's CGU guidance](https://www.francenum.gouv.fr/guides-et-conseils/developpement-commercial/site-web/rediger-des-conditions-generales-dutilisation), consulted September 30, 2026. Mandatory-rights language was informed by the [European Commission's explanation of unfair contract terms](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/consumer-contract-law/unfair-contract-terms-directive_en). These sources are authoring references, not evidence of legal approval or jurisdiction-specific completeness. A subsequent user request added an English/French selector and French translations of the Terms and Privacy pages. Translating this information does not establish market-specific legal completeness or legal approval.

The Spotify limitation carries forward the existing README and `docs/spotapi-assessment.md`; this visual task did not enable that integration or establish permission for AI use.
