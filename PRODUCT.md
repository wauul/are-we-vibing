# Are We Vibing

## Platform
web

The same Next.js interface also runs in a Capacitor Android shell.

## Users
Inferred from the existing product: friends, couples and pairs comparing music taste, sharing a playful result and discovering songs. Mobile sharing is a primary scene. Exact demographics are unconfirmed.

## Product Purpose
Two people contribute music to one persistent session. AI returns a subjective score, summaries, verdict, recommendations and participant awards.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Manual picks and YouTube playlists are supported. Experimental Spotify public-playlist import uses anonymous embed metadata without Spotify credentials; its AI-use terms restriction remains unresolved. Guests use bearer links. Optional Google accounts add profiles, friendships and direct invitations. YouTube playback is independent. Exported PNG cards download on web or save to Android Gallery. Android adds native sign-in, notifications and app links; iOS is absent. Preserve routes, APIs, database records, permissions and native bridges. Service credentials are absent locally.

## Brand Commitments
